01 · LOGOmudassar.work

the logo

A split circle holding a bold lowercase ms. Half solid, half open: the work and the space around it. The wordmark pairs a light mudassar with a bold .work.

Primary logo. Use on light backgrounds.

Reversed. White version for dark backgrounds.

Badge only. Avatars, favicon, stamps.

02 · USAGEmudassar.work

space & size

xx

Clear space. Keep a margin of x around the logo, where x is half the badge's radius. Nothing enters this area.

mark · 16 px / 6 mm
full · 120 px / 30 mm wide

Minimum size. Below these sizes switch to the badge on its own and keep it on a plain background.

Do use black or white only.

Do place on plain, high-contrast backgrounds.

Don't stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows.

Don't recolor, or place on busy photos.
