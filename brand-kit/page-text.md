## About (long)

Cricket Sport brings you cricket scores, results and milestones as they happen.

What we post:
- Toss and live score updates for men's international and top-team matches
- Final results with the top scorer and top wicket taker
- Centuries, five-wicket hauls and record moments
- Today's matches, polls and weekly tables

Every score card is made by us from match data. Tell us in the comments who you think wins today.

Scores are updated automatically and may lag behind the ground by a few minutes.
